LAB PIKO Uploader

About

LAB PIKO Uploader is a private, single-user application operated by the owner of the LAB PIKO YouTube channel (youtube.com/@LabPiko). It is used only to upload the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

Privacy Policy

The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube account via Google OAuth, using the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes.

The only data stored is the OAuth refresh token, kept securely on the owner's own systems and used solely to perform uploads.

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data of any third party. It uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Effective date: 16 November 2026.
